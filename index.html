Mouse, touch and keyboard

Mouse Drag left or right to turn the field. Drag up or down to tilt. Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. In fullscreen, use the wheel to zoom.

Touch Swipe sideways to turn and vertically to scroll. Tap + or − to zoom. In fullscreen, pinch to zoom or move two fingers to pan.

Keyboard Focus the scan. Arrow keys rotate, Shift + arrows pan, and + or − zoom. F centers the highlighted plant. R resets the view.

How the scanner samples a crop

Where rays land

Grid layouts scatter rays around evenly spaced samples. Lines and sweeps change the gaps along and across rows. Rings use the selected sensor’s channel and column counts, up to the ray budget. Each ray records the highest surface at its sample. Beam calibration, intensity, motion and multiple returns require a richer sensor model.
