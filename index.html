How the scanner samples a crop

Grid profiles distribute jittered samples over the plot. Line and sweep profiles change coverage along and across rows. Ring layouts use angular columns and channels, capped by the ray budget. The synthetic renderer takes the highest surface at each sample. Hardware-inspired presets reproduce selected channel and column counts. Inside the training shed

Grow the answer key

Each generated plant has a bounding box. Its horizontal midpoint gives the scorer a known plant center.

Learn from local peaks

The trainer finds height peaks in a 5 cm grid and measures 13 clues around each peak. Training updates model weights so peaks near plant centers receive higher scores.

Scout unfamiliar ground

New seeds supply the validation fields. Precision counts how often a pin finds a plant. Recall counts how many plants receive a pin.
