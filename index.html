Field school

What does training do?

Grow the answer key

The generator puts leaves around each plant and records its bounding box. The box midpoint gives us a known center to learn from.

Learn which peaks are plants

The detector finds height peaks in a top-down grid. It measures 13 clues around each peak, including height, shape and point density. Training adjusts weights to give peaks near a plant a higher score.

Scout unfamiliar ground

Validation uses different field seeds. Precision tells you how many pins found plants. Recall tells you how many plants got a pin. F1 balances both at 7.5, 10 and 15 cm.

In the training shed, compare the four-unit neural network with the logistic baseline. Use the field selector and map overlay to see which plants disappear between the peak, score and spacing stages.
